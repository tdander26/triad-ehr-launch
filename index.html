10Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Email doc@drtoddanderson.com. Todd reads every one.

Is this only for Applied Kinesiology practices?

No. Triad is for cash-based functional and integrative practices: functional medicine, chiropractic, naturopathic and AK. Functional-range labs, supplement plans to checkout, dictation and the progress picture work the same for everyone. And if you practice AK, you also get structured findings for valves, challenges, cranial faults and offenders, which is where Triad started.

What about my data? I can't lose my patient history.

We move it for you, free. Triad imports patients, appointments and chart-note files from your current EHR's export, and anything it can't match to exactly one patient is listed for review instead of guessed. Leaving is never a trap: Settings has a full export of every chart, note and document.

How much does it cost?

Founding target pricing is $79 per provider per month (Foundation) or $129 with voice dictation and AI drafts (Professional). Month-to-month, no add-on stacking. Founding practices lock in a lower rate for life, and final numbers get set honestly with our first practices.

I'm on a low-cost EHR already. Why pay more?

Fair question. On low-cost generalist software you're not overpaying, you're under-served: lab patterns, protocols and muscle tests still go into a free-text box, and add-ons creep the price back up. Triad's price buys structured charting (AK included), the progress picture and functional-range labs. If it isn't obviously better for how you practice, don't switch.

Is it actually HIPAA secure?

We built for it from day one. Triad runs on Google Cloud under a signed BAA, and dictation (AssemblyAI) and patient email (Amazon SES) are each under their own. Triad EHR LLC signs a BAA with your practice before your data comes in. We never say "HIPAA certified," because no such certification exists.

Can I order labs from Triad?

Today Triad tracks the labs you order, schedules rechecks, and imports results from PDF (tuned for LabCorp reports and DUTCH hormone reports). Direct electronic lab ordering, with results flowing straight into the chart, is in development.

Will learning new software wreck my schedule?

The single screen is the training. Your most-used patterns get built into macros during setup, a checklist walks your staff through the rest, and Help has a "What's new" list so no update is a surprise.

You're a small company. Will you actually be around?

Real question, real answer. Triad isn't a prototype betting on your data. It runs a live practice every day, owned by Triad EHR LLC, a Minnesota company. The Founding Cohort de-risks it anyway: free first weeks, month-to-month, and a full export you can take anytime.
